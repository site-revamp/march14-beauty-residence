# March14 Beauty Residence – Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

March14 Beauty Residence is a French-inspired luxury beauty destination located in Al Quoz, Dubai. Founded by Ruby Barreau, the business offers specialist haircare, nail artistry, lash and brow treatments, spray tanning and personalised beauty experiences.

The redesigned homepage presents March14 as a premium beauty residence rather than a conventional salon. It focuses on international beauty expertise, specialist services, personalised treatments and the brand's distinctive French heritage.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Consistent Website Structure and Service Presentation

- Establish a unified visual and navigation structure across the homepage and service pages.
- Organise the four main beauty studios into a consistent service hierarchy.
- Align typography, layouts and service descriptions with the homepage design system.

**Problem addressed:** The previous website used inconsistent layouts and navigation structures across its homepage and Services page, creating a disconnected browsing experience.

### 2. Clearer Appointment Booking Journey

- Improve the presentation of bookable beauty service categories.
- Ensure Body & Details services are accounted for alongside Hair, Nails, Lashes & Brows and Spray Tanning.
- Provide clearer pathways for customers to book treatments or enquire about services requiring consultation.

**Problem addressed:** The previous booking journey did not clearly include every service category promoted elsewhere on the website, potentially creating confusion for customers.

### 3. More Accurate and Credible Website Content

- Update outdated year references across website content.
- Prioritise verifiable brand differentiators over unsupported promotional claims.
- Review treatment descriptions and performance-related statements for accuracy.
- Maintain consistency between homepage messaging, service information and contact details.

**Problem addressed:** Outdated references and unsupported claims weakened content credibility and created inconsistencies across the website.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Strengthened the luxury beauty positioning through a more refined and cohesive visual direction.
- **Layout:** Improved content hierarchy and the presentation of the four specialist beauty studios.
- **User Experience:** Created clearer pathways for visitors to explore services and move towards appointment enquiries.
- **Brand Consistency:** Established a more unified presentation of March14's French heritage, specialist expertise and premium positioning.
- **Content Structure:** Improved the organisation of service information to make the homepage easier to navigate and understand.

## Overall Result

The redesigned homepage positions March14 Beauty Residence as a premium, internationally inspired beauty destination in Dubai. The improvements focus on clearer service communication, a more consistent website experience and stronger content credibility while reinforcing the brand's specialist expertise and luxury identity.
