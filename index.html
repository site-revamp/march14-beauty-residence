Founder story · Ruby Barreau
The person behind the residence

A world of beauty, shaped by Ruby.

March14 was founded by Ruby Barreau, a French beauty entrepreneur whose career spans celebrity beauty work, spray tanning and international beauty-team consultancy.

Her experience working with public figures and beauty teams at events including the Met Gala and Cannes Film Festival informs the standards and creative point of view behind the residence.

“Every artist, formula and detail should have a reason to be here.”
